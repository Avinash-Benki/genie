# MF & Goal Allocator

ALWAYS read ../../investor-context.md first. Run market-pulse before equity deployment.

## Step 1 — Bucket by horizon (non-negotiable)
| Horizon to goal | Allowed instruments |
|---|---|
| < 12 months | Arbitrage funds, liquid funds, ultra-short debt, FDs laddered to the goal date. ZERO equity. |
| 1–3 years | Above + balanced advantage funds (BAF), conservative hybrid. |
| 3–5 years | BAF, flexi-cap, index funds; ≤ 20% mid/small. |
| 5+ years | Full equity architecture: ~50% index, ~30% flexi-cap, ~10% mid, ~10% small (cap small at 10%). |

## Step 2 — Post-tax math (30% slab, new regime — VERIFY current FY rates via search)
- Arbitrage funds: equity taxation; <12m STCG ≈ 20.8% → typically the best post-tax short-term vehicle.
- Debt/liquid/ultra-short bought after Apr-2023, and FDs: slab (~31.2%) on all gains.
- Always compare POST-TAX yields, never headline. Show the math in the answer.

## Step 3 — Fund screening rules
- Direct plans only. Expense ratio: <0.5% debt/arbitrage, <1% active equity, <0.2% index.
- EXIT LOAD vs horizon check is mandatory (Parag Parikh 2%/365-day lesson — a great fund can be the wrong vehicle for a short horizon).
- AUM sanity: arbitrage/liquid prefer large AUM; small-cap prefer not-too-large.
- Search CURRENT NAV, 1Y/3Y returns, exit load, ER before naming any fund. Cite.

## Step 4 — Deployment mechanics
- Lump sums: split into 2–3 tranches with NAMED triggers (index level, crude level, event date) — write the triggers in the answer.
- Dated goals: FD ladder maturing just BEFORE the expense date; consolidate to liquid fund 4–6 weeks before the goal.
- Idle-cash rule: surplus goes to liquid/ultra-short the week it arrives (see lessons.md — the ₹2.3L pause lesson).

## Output format
Allocation table (instrument | amount | % | rationale | when) → post-tax math → execution steps per platform (Groww flow) → thresholds that would change the plan.
